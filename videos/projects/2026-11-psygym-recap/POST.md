## Text

Novawerk's third project, PSYGYM, is delivered. 🎉

It's our first voice agent. PSYGYM helps counselors who don't have much real experience yet get some, safely, before they leave university. You hold a practice session with an AI client by voice, and a separate supervisor model tells you what you could do better.

The idea came to us as a pitch. Over six weeks we helped its founder, a psychology PhD with deep knowledge of the field and no engineering or product background, turn it into a real app. It's now on the App Store.

Shipping a mental health app in Europe is hard. To get privacy and compliance right before launch, and to clear the way for whatever the team does next, we did far more work than building the app itself, from how voice data is handled to the paperwork around it.

Along the way, we engineers learned what being a counselor actually means. That part was fascinating.

This is why Novawerk exists. The question I get asked most is: how do you make money? And if you don't, what's in it for you, and how does it last?

It's a hard question to answer. Without an obvious payoff, it can look like we have a hidden motive. 😄

But there really are two reasons.

First, we want to show that free lunches exist, and so does plain goodwill between people. If we want the world to get better, we should do what we can to make it better.

Second, it's fun! We meet people from other fields, use what we know to solve all kinds of problems, and get to learn, talk and try new things.

Compared with real charity work, we still have a long way to go, and we may not have helped that many people yet. But this is what we can do in our spare time, in a way we're good at and enjoy. So we want to keep doing it.

Next up: QM Xiaoku. Stay tuned!

Thanks for reading. If you have an idea you want to build and no tech team, or you just want to talk, don't hesitate to reach out.

Our next slot opens in January. If you organise events in Berlin and need any technical help, get in touch. We don't charge for it!
