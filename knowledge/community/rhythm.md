# Community rhythm

Recurring events that hold the community together. The site phrases the
overall cadence as:

> *Open day every 3rd Sunday.*

## Event types

| Type        | Format          | Purpose                                                                  |
|-------------|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Open House  | In-person       | Short demos from every active project. The flagship monthly gathering.   |
| Workshop    | In-person       | Help turn *"I want to make X"* into a weekly plan (i.e. into MAUs).      |
| Online AMA  | Online          | Topic-driven conversations — e.g. *launching a non-profit without burning cash*. |

## Cadence

- **Open House** — third Sunday of every month.
- **Workshops** — roughly monthly, usually a Saturday near the open day.
- **Online AMAs** — opportunistic, scheduled around topics the community is
  actively wrestling with.
