# Channels and contact

How to reach Novawerk and where the community lives online.

## Primary contact

- **Email** — `info@novawerk.io` (canonical; both inbound contact and
  press use the same address)
- **Default outbound from** — `Novawerk <info@novawerk.io>` (set via
  `CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL`)
- **Contact form** — `novawerk.io/community#contact` → `POST /api/contact`
- **Newsletter** — `novawerk.io/community#newsletter` → `POST /api/newsletter`

> Note: older marketing materials use `.org` variants of this address.
> Those are stale — always use `info@novawerk.io`.
