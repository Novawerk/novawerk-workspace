# Principle 02 — Build, Learn, Together

> An open community where people of all backgrounds learn by building real things.

## Long form (canonical)

Novawerk is an open community where people come together to learn, build, and
grow through practical collaboration and real projects. We welcome individuals
from different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels who share a desire
to create positive change together.

## Implications

- **Skills are a side-effect of the project.** So are friendships. We don't
  run skill-building as its own activity — we build real things, and the
  skills follow.
- **Background, experience, and skill level matter less than intent.** The
  filter is whether you want to contribute, not what you already know.
- **Pairing is normal.** New contributors are paired with experienced ones on
  real work, not given exercises.
