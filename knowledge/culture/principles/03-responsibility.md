# Principle 03 — Responsibility for Positive Change

> Everyone shares the responsibility to contribute. Indifference is the only failure.

## Long form (canonical)

We believe everyone shares a responsibility to contribute positively to
society through their actions, ideas, and participation. Positive change
begins when people choose to care, contribute, and take responsibility
instead of remaining indifferent or inactive.

## The headline statement

The community page leads with this principle as a single line:

> *Indifference is the only failure.*

It's followed by:

> You don't have to wait until you're ready. You don't have to stockpile
> contacts, skills, time, or courage first. You only have to decide to start
> — and from that moment, this thing is already different.
