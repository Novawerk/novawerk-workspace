# Community pledge

Four lines printed in a bordered card on the `/community` page. They are not
a legal agreement, but they are the closest thing Novawerk has to a code of
conduct in members' own words.

## The pledge

1. I'll show up with something concrete, not just an idea.
2. I'll let others see my progress — and look at theirs.
3. I'll value what got done over what got said.
4. I accept failure. I refuse indifference.

## How it's used

The pledge is presented to anyone considering joining. It is meant to be
read out loud — short enough that you finish it before you talk yourself
out of it. It encodes the third principle (*Responsibility for Positive
Change*) at the level of individual behaviour.
