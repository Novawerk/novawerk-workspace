# Principle 01 — Purposeful Innovation

> Innovation should create real positive change — not chase trends or quick wins.

## Long form (canonical)

We believe innovation should create real positive change for people and
society. Rather than following short-term trends or simple replication, we
value meaningful ideas, thoughtful execution, and long-term contribution
through real action.

## The test

The question we care about: *when this thing exists, is the world a little
better?* If the honest answer is "I don't know yet," the project is fine —
keep going. If the honest answer is "no, but it might trend," the project
isn't a Novawerk project.
