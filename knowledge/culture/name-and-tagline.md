# Name, tagline, and type

Novawerk is an **open, non-profit community hub**, established in **2025**. The
canonical tagline is *An open workshop for early ideas.* It sits above the headline
on the home page: the tagline says what we are, the headline says what we do.
Until October 2026 the tagline was *Not profitable, but meaningful.*

## Details

- **Name** — Novawerk (always capitalised this way; not *NovaWerk* or *Nova Werk*).
- **Tagline** — *An open workshop for early ideas.*
- **Headline** — We help ideas take their first step.
- **One-line description** — An open, non-profit community hub focused on creating
  real positive change in society through collaboration, creativity, and action.
- **Type** — Non-profit community. Not a company, accelerator, incubator, or
  studio. The framing on the site is *workshop*, not *platform*.
- **Founded** — 2025.
- **Primary domain** — `novawerk.io`.
- **Brand mark** — the Nova Star (a spinning star icon, used as the favicon and
  inline accent throughout the site).
