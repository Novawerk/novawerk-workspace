01 / Context

Novawerk is a workshop,
not a startup.

Open. Non-profit. Community.

An open hub for people turning meaningful ideas into positive change. Founded 2025. Tagline: An open workshop for early ideas.

The brand has to feel that.

It can't read corporate, accelerator, or tech-bro. The visual system is plain-spoken, warm at the edges, intentional in motion.

02 / The mark

The Nova Star.

A four-pointed star with sharp pinches — outer points at N/E/S/W, inner arms tightly compressed near the centre.

It functions as favicon, inline accent, and the principle-card icon throughout Novawerk surfaces.

03 / Evolution

From tile, to star, to depth.

v0 — Favicon tile

Rounded green tile with a cream star. Reads as favicon, not as logo.

v1 — Bare star

Removed the tile. Cleaner — but flat. No sense of gravity.

v2 — Depth core

Two concentric layers. The star feels like it has a centre — a somewhere it's pulling toward.

04 / The question

Is it contracting, or is it expanding?

A mark that holds the question is more interesting than one that answers it. The deeper-green core reads as recession; the breathing motion reads as arrival. Both are correct.

05 / Construction

Two paths. One mark.

Both stars share the same four-point geometry. The core is 28% the size of the outer star, same rotation, deeper green.

// Outer star — 100×100 viewBox
M50 0  L55 45  L100 50  L55 55
L50 100  L45 55  L0 50  L45 45  Z

// Inner core — 28% scale
M50 36  L51.4 48.6  L64 50  L51.4 51.4
L50 64  L48.6 51.4  L36 50  L48.6 48.6  Z

Below 16 px the core is dropped — single solid star for legibility.

07 / Mono

In one ink, the core becomes a cutout.

Same construction, different reading.

In colour, the dark core reads as depth. In mono, it reads as a hole — the event horizon's edge.

Use for single-ink print, partner contexts, dark UIs, embroidery, and accessibility fallbacks.

09 / Why this type

Industrial. Structured.
The "werk" in the name.

Geometric grotesque

Modern grotesque with character. Reads as confident, builderly, contemporary — not corporate-tech.

Display weight

800 gives the wordmark mass without going slab-blocky. Holds up at favicon size and on a billboard.

Free + accessible

Ships from Fontshare under a commercial-free licence. No licensing friction for partners or community.

Considered and rejected: Inter Tight (too neutral), Fraunces (editorial, too soft for "werk").

11 / Type system

Two families, three voices.

RoleFamilyWeightWhere
Display Cabinet Grotesk 700 · 800 Wordmark, hero lines, section openers
Body Inter 400 · 500 Paragraphs, UI labels, captions
accent Inter italic 400 · 500 One word per sentence — the meaning-bearer

No all-caps headlines. Display sets tight (−2 to −3.5px letter-spacing). Italic accent only ever pairs with --color-accent.

12 / Shape

The mark is sharp.
Everything around it gets soft.

Mark

Sharp, geometric, never enclosed

Cards

16 px radius — M3 "large"

Button →

Pills

999 px — for CTAs and pills

The contrast is the point: the mark's eight tips against soft surroundings. Don't enclose the mark. Don't square-corner the cards.

13 / Motion

Springy, not snappy.

Star spin — 14 s linear

The signature. For "this is the brand speaking" moments — hero loaders, decorative low-opacity marks.

UI transitions — 180–240 ms

cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1) — chrome, dropdowns, modals.

Emphasis spring — 280–360 ms

cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1) — confirm states, hero entrance, the accent word landing.

All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — instant transitions, no spin.

14 / Lineage

Material 3 Expressive — five elements.

M3 Expressive's thesis: design that makes you feel something, achieved through deliberate use of these five. The Novawerk VI inherits the framework, not the look.

Colour

Strategic vibrancy. One loud accent per surface.

Shape

Sharp mark against soft container shapes.

Size

Few sizes, big contrast — display vs body.

Motion

Spring easing for emphasis. The 14 s spin signature.

Containment

Cards over panels. Grouping by shape, not by line.

Reference: m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-expressive

15 / Layout

One hero. One accent. The rest reads quietly.

Generous vertical rhythm

96–128 px section padding on desktop, 48–64 px on mobile. Breathing room is part of the brand.

Asymmetry is allowed

Don't force a centred lockup if the composition wants left-anchored. Off-centre is more expressive.

Cards over banded sections

Group related content in soft-edged cards (16 px radius) on cream — not full-width colour bands.

Earn the loud moment

Each surface gets one moment of full brand voice — a loud headline, an accent block, the spinning mark. Pick that moment carefully.

16 / Do

Do these.

✓ Lockup in headers

Horizontal lockup for full surfaces; mark alone for compact contexts (favicon, avatar, inline accent).

✓ Cream as a colour

Not a default. #efe9d8 is the room the brand lives in.

✓ Sharp mark, soft UI

Pair the eight tips with cards (16 px) and pills (999 px). Contrast is the read.

✓ Animate sparingly

Spring easings, short durations, reduced-motion fallback. The spin earns attention by being rare.
