# Meetings and ceremonies

The meetings we run to keep Novawerk moving, and the principles that keep them
worth attending. We aim for the fewest meetings that keep us aligned — each one
with a clear purpose, the right people, and a written outcome.

## The meetings

| Meeting | Expectation | Cadence / length | Participants |
|---------|-------------|------------------|--------------|
| **All-hands** | Get together, raise and clear blockers, check timeline progress, ideate, coordinate, talk funding | Fortnightly / 60 min | All core members |
| **Development daily** | Project-oriented check-in: what's done, what's next, what's blocked | Every 2 days / 15 min | Project participants |
| **Development review + planning** | Review the project release candidate, plan the next stretch | Every 2 weeks / 45 min | Project participants |
| **Project kick-off / wrap-up** | Start or close out a project, and assign the work | Ad-hoc, max 2 per project / 45 min each | Project participants |
| **Team building — offsite** | Casual offsite, drinks and snacks, no agenda | Every 6 weeks / 2 hours | All members |

## Principles

These hold for every meeting on the list.

- **Async first.** Development daily is async by default — post your update. We
  call a sync only when a blocker needs discussion.
- **On time, in time.** We start on time, keep to the time box, and end on time.
- **Always recorded.** Every meeting is recorded so people who miss it can catch
  up.
- **Pre-read for All-hands.** All-hands needs a must-have agenda and a one-pager
  pre-read shared before the meeting — your ideation and proposed topics.
  Everyone reads it beforehand so we jump straight into the discussion.
- **Outcome-oriented.** Every meeting produces a recorded doc and a
  call-to-action. No outcome, no point.
- **Everyone raises ideas.** Speaking up is encouraged, from anyone, on any
  topic.

## Review

We review how these meetings are working every quarter. Two questions:

1. Is the design working as we expected?
2. What needs adjusting — cadence, length, participants, or format?

We make the call, write it down here, and update the doc.
