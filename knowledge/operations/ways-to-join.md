# Ways to join

There is no single front door. People come in through different surfaces,
take their first step at different speeds, and grow into different
[roles](./titles-and-roles.md) on the projects they join.

## What's expected before doing anything

Anyone joining is expected to read the
[community pledge](../culture/principles/pledge.md) — four lines that
encode how Novawerk members agree to show up for each other.

## How to actually start

- **Contribute to our tools** at `novawerk.io/contribute`. Anyone can pick an
  open task on the software Novawerk runs on (the site, the admin panel, the
  API, the agents) at `novawerk.io/tasks`, propose a plan, and ship it. Signing
  in (GitHub, Google or an emailed code) makes your Novawerk account: you are a
  member. Merged work earns Werk, which is recognition only: never money, no
  levels.
- Have an idea you want to start? Pitch it at `novawerk.io/submit`. When we
  accept the pitch, you join the project's team as its Initiator, and you sign
  in with the email you pitched from; the admin panel then shows you the
  project.
- Subscribe to the monthly newsletter in the footer of every page.
- Or just show up at the next [open day](../community/rhythm.md).

We closed applications to join (`novawerk.io/join`) in October 2026: we don't
have the people to mentor newcomers through whole projects. Old links lead to
`/contribute`.

## Members and maintainers

Everyone is a member, with one account for the task board, the admin panel and
the team chat. There are no tiers. Maintainers are the core team who run
Novawerk: they pick proposals, accept suggestions, moderate, approve outgoing
email and see the whole admin panel. Being on a project's team is not a rank:
it opens that project in the admin panel. Members take roles on the projects
they join — see [members and project roles](./titles-and-roles.md).
"Contributors" is still the word for the people on the wall at
`/contributors`: those who have built something with us.
