# How we work

Every Novawerk project moves through four steps. They are not phases on a
Gantt chart — projects loop through them many times — but they are the four
verbs that describe what a contributor is actually doing in any given week.

## The four steps

| #  | Step           | What it means                                                                                              |
|----|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 01 | Validate       | Sharpen a vague idea into a clear problem statement worth solving.                                         |
| 02 | Break Down     | Slice ambition into milestones small enough to ship next week.                                             |
| 03 | Collaborate    | Find designers, engineers, researchers and community folk who complete your team.                          |
| 04 | Build for Real | We don't make demos. We make things that have to be handed over.                                           |

## Why "for real"

Step 4 is the one most communities skip. A demo answers *can we do this?* A
real, handed-over thing answers *did we actually do it, and does it work
when we let go?* Novawerk projects are graded by step 4. A great validation
that never gets built does not count.
