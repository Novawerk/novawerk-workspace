# Brand voice

How Novawerk speaks, in copy and in conversation.

## Tone

- Direct, plain, low-jargon. Sentences land short.
- Speak as the community, not about it: "we", present tense.
- Concrete over abstract. Use the artifact, not the buzzword.

## Visual accents in copy

Italicised accent words (`<span class="italic text-accent">…</span>`) are used
sparingly to mark *the* word that carries the meaning of the line. Recurring
accent words on the site:

> *together · meaningful · building · us · making · real positive change ·
> Let's make it real. · workshop*

One accent per sentence, at most.

## Words to avoid

These appear in pitch decks but never on Novawerk surfaces:

- leverage
- ecosystem
- stakeholders
- (and any marketing puffery in the same family — "synergy", "disrupt",
  "transform" used as a verb-on-its-own)
